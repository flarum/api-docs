DarkBodyBackground  

class DarkBodyBackground

The dark-mode body background, hsl(secondary-hue, min(20%, secondary-sat), 10%), for the critical CSS and the standalone pages, which have no compiled stylesheet, together with the colour maths needed to keep their text readable against it.

less/common/variables.less declares the same formula for the forum's own CSS. DarkBodyBackgroundTest compiles @body-bg-dark and compares it, so the two cannot drift apart.

at line 90
static float luminance(string $hex)

The background luminance to measure light text against: the brighter of the declared colour and the bytes it rounds to.

The declared hsl() resolves to channels that are not whole bytes, so a colour can reach the minimum contrast against one model and miss it against the other. The brighter of the two is the worst case for light text on the dark background.
